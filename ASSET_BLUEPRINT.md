# Malabar House / shared asset specification

The front elevation supplied with the brief is the visual source of truth. Its defining features are a symmetrical off-white two-storey facade, a deep recessed central arch and stair, three openings in each wing, continuous timber verandas, low front veranda roofs, broad terracotta upper roofs, and a taller timber-braced central gable. The unpictured sides, back, and interior are deliberately restrained extensions of that elevation.

## Coordinate contract (metres)

- Origin: centre of the building at exterior grade. `+X` is right when looking at the front, `+Y` is up, `+Z` points out of the front entrance. Symmetry plane: `X = 0`.
- Overall masonry/plinth footprint: `X = -17..17`, `Z = -8.5..8.5` (34 x 17 m). No module may change it.
- Front room wall: `Z = 5.7`. Front verandas occupy `Z = 5.7..8.5`; the central arch projects to `Z = 8.7`, and the exterior steps project to `Z = 12.85`.
- Exterior wall: 0.34 m; gallery portal fragments: 0.18 m; plinth/floor datum: 0.75 m; first-floor datum: 4.8 m; upper wall top: 8.4 m; highest gable/finial: approximately 14.6 m.
- Museum interior (Agent 06): each floor is **one open hall** with **no interior partitions at all** — no room dividers, portal walls or cross walls. The only things standing inside the envelope are the U-stair with its guards and the structural arcade below.
- Bearing walls (Agent 02, `structure.ts` exports `BEARING_WALL`): full solid masonry walls from edge to edge (`Z = -8.15..5.53`) along `|X| = 4.89`, 300 mm thick, on both ground and upper floors. Each wall has only one clean, cased doorway opening (`Z = 1.65`, 1.6 m wide, 2.6 m high with teak casing) on the left and right side to enter into the open wing halls.
- Stair access: the two bearing-wall doorways at `Z = 1.65` sit in front of the stair foot (`Z = -0.45`) on both floors, so the central bay connects straight into each hall at floor and first-floor level; nothing (rail, flight or wall) stands in a doorway.
- Window schedule (raised for museum display): ground sills 1.38 m above the 0.75 m floor (about 2.13 m above grade, 1.38 m above the plinth band), heights around 1.3 m; rear/side ground lights 1.32 m high (top ~4.09, clear of the slab). Upper sills 1.02 m (clear of the 1.08 m handrail), front heights 1.28 m tucked between the spandrel (top 4.58) and the eave bearer underside (8.93); rear/side upper lights 1.55 m (top 7.37). All windows read as glazed clerestories with closed louvred shutters.
- Entrance axis: `X = 0`. Main door 2.35 m wide, front arch clear width 3.12 m, stair 4.24 m wide with five 0.15 m risers and 0.33 m treads (bottom nosing `Z = 12.85`, stair head `Z = 11.20`). Wings use opening centres at `|X| = 7.25, 11.05, 14.85 m`. The middle opening of each wing is a veranda door.
- Entrance porch (Agent 03, `entrance.ts` exports `ENTRANCE`): the podium runs the full 10.3 m width forward from `Z = 8.5` to `Z = 11.20`, so the porch is a walkable veranda. Two **free-standing** corner columns (240 mm radius) stand on it at `Z = 10.15`, `|X| = 4.70`, leaving 0.95 m of clear passage behind them to the arch wall face (`Z = 8.96`); the former middle pair is removed so the centre of the porch is fully open. They carry the 9.4 m porch architrave (`Y = 4.20..4.40`, `Z = 8.90..10.55`) and cornice (`Y = 4.40..4.58`, `Z = 8.90..10.68`), whose back edge is embedded in the arch wall. Vertical stack on the axis: arch opening crown 3.86, continuous white archivolt crown 4.08 (13 even voussoirs, no keystone block), cornice top 4.58 = balcony deck underside.
- Heritage wall lanterns (`lanterns.ts`, Agents 03/05/08): one lantern design, built once in local space (wall face `z = 0`, arm axis `y = 0`) and cloned to every mount, so all share geometry. Each is a continuous chain: scrolled `lampIron` backplate (30 mm off the wall) → brass rosette + arm collar → 270 mm bracket arm → brass joint collar → base pan sitting directly on the arm top (drip lip clear) → four corner posts with four `lampGlass` panes → brass oil reservoir and emissive flame → cap ring → pyramidal cap → stem and ball finial. A diagonal brace ties the arm back to the plate. **19 mounts**: 2 flanking the entrance arch at `|X| = 2.95`, arm axis `Y = 3.50` (top 4.02, 0.18 m clear of the architrave soffit); 8 on the ground veranda and 8 on the first-floor veranda, one on each solid pier between the front openings (`|X| = 5.90, 9.11, 12.99, 16.06` per side), arm axis `Y = 2.45` and `Y = 6.50`; and 1 centred over the first-floor balcony door on the entrance axis, arm axis `Y = 8.20` (backplate foot 7.87, 0.15 m above the door head moulding top of 7.72, on the solid lintel wall 7.50..8.40 that continues into the attic wall). All mount on the front wall face `Z = 5.87` and project to `Z = 6.34`, clear of the railing line (8.47). `lampGlass` (`18`) is emissive and `lampIron` (`19`) is aged dark bronze.
- Night mode (viewer only, never exported): the viewer adds one warm point light at each lantern flame, with zero intensity in daylight. Toggling night mode drops the sun to a cool 0.22 moonlight, cuts the fill and ambient, darkens the sky and fog, then raises all 19 lamp lights to 3.4 and boosts the emissive lamp glass.
- Hall chandeliers (`chandeliers.ts`): four matching eight-arm antique-brass fixtures, one centred in each west/east hall on each floor at `X = +/-10.85`, `Z = -1.315`. Ground roses touch the slab soffit at `Y = 4.54`; upper roses touch the plaster ceiling at `Y = 8.375`. Each is about 1.8 m wide with a drop below 1.15 m, preserving at least 2.4 m walking clearance. A continuous ceiling rose, suspension stem, turned centre, curved arms, brass drip dishes, warm opal lotus shades and attached faceted drops form a connected fixture. Geometry is merged by material once and shared by all four fixtures; the model and emissive materials are included in GLB exports.
- Hall illumination: one warm, shadowed point light represents the eight bulbs of each chandelier (four lights total), with cached 256/512-pixel shadow faces rather than per-frame shadow rendering. Interior lights remain gently on by day and brighten in Night mode. Upper-hall lights follow the upper-floor cutaway visibility. The ZIP includes `hall-lighting.json` with ceiling mounts, emitter offsets and day/night settings; preview lights remain separate from the asset geometry.
- Central upper balcony (Agent 05, `verandas.ts` exports `BALCONY`): the deck extends over the whole entrance porch, `|X| <= 5.15`, `Z = 8.5..10.68`, `Y = 4.58..4.80`, bearing directly on the porch cornice. An 80 mm teak floor-edge kerb (top 4.85, matching the wing floor edges) wraps the front and both sides flush with the cream cornice faces. Four 270 mm roof posts at `|X| = 1.15, 3.45`, `Z = 9.08` carry the gable eave bearer and stand on the deck. The front railing extends across the entire front edge (`Z = 10.50`) with corner newels at `|X| = 4.96` (only 11 cm from the side deck kerb), eliminating empty space on the left and right. Side return railings run along `|X| = 4.96` from `Z = 10.50` back to `Z = 8.38`, then a short link bay connects to the first wing post at `|X| = 5.40`. The stair balustrade is a slim sloped handrail (90 x 85 mm) whose pitch equals the stair pitch, with one turned 64 mm baluster centred on each tread and a turned newel at each end; `|X| = 2.03`, clear of both the step edge and the columns.
- Front posts: `|X| = 5.4, 9.2, 13, 16.83 m`, all **round** (260 mm shafts, 280 mm corners, tapering 10 percent). Central upper balcony: four round 270 mm roof posts at `|X| = 1.15, 3.45 m`, open centre, standing on the deck that extends over the entrance porch; front railing spans edge-to-edge with corner newels at `|X| = 4.96 m`. Railings tenon 40 mm into the turned shafts and end on round newels. Every column is built by `columns.ts`: floor → moulded round base → tapered shaft → neck bead + bell capital → round abacus → beam underside. The two entrance columns are free-standing round corner columns (240 mm radius) on the porch podium, detached from the arch wall; exterior stair balusters, stair newels and interior stair uprights are turned round members. Each building front corner has a 340 mm masonry pier closing the former wall notch.
- Roof (Agent 07, all planes 30 degrees except the intentionally steeper raised gable cheeks, 150 mm tile slab, 1.0-1.1 m eaves at `Z = +/-9.6`, `X = +/-17.98`): main roof is two equal hipped ranges with ridges at `Y = 12.1`, `Z = +/-3.62`, meeting in one level valley at `Z = 0`, `Y = 10.0` that discharges onto the end hips. Continuous eave at `Y = 8.65` on all four sides, bearing on the upper veranda bearers (top 9.11) and the knee walls. Central gablet: a 7.83 m-wide timber screen on the front wall line `Z = 5.7` (base 10.90, peak 14.2) with 40 degree cheeks. Gable face: one strong triangular timber frame on the roof pitch (170 x 150 mm verge members) with clay rolls continuing the ridge language around both verges, a base sill on the roof, a single centred post (150 mm) from base to ridge with squared base/head blocks, five evenly spaced horizontal ventilation louvers, two small carved spandrels flanking the post near the peak, and a traditional finial (square plinth, turned neck, cone tip) at the apex. The two main gable tile planes are triangles from the front apex `(0, 14.2, 5.7)` to the shared main-ridge point `(0, 12.1, 3.62)` and their main-slope valley starts `(X = +/-3.92, Y = 10.90, Z = 5.7)`. Main front tiles are clipped on exactly those valley lines and all three ridges meet at one mitred junction. The raised 30 degree entrance apron remains at `Y = 9.525` on its balcony bearer and reaches the narrower apron top (`|X| = 2.88`, `Y = 11.78`, `Z = 5.7`). On each side, two terracotta transition facets bridge the raised apron to the lower wing plane: raised-eave → wing-eave → main valley, then raised-eave → main valley → apron top. Their boundaries are finished by a raised-apron hip, a lower valley cap, front sill flashing, and matching hip/valley rafters directly below. This replaces the former dark folded wedge; every tile edge is shared with the apron, main slope, gable screen, or fascia. There is one front timber screen, no rear gable screen, and the descending gable ridge cap and both continuous valley rolls stop against the same covered main-ridge junction. Ground skirt: one continuous roof per wing that wraps the veranda corner. The front plane runs from the entrance arch wall (`|X| = 5.15`) to the corner; the side plane runs down the end of the veranda from the building front face (`Z = 5.87`) to the corner. Both are 30 degrees and meet in a true 45 degree hip with equal 0.715 m overhang. The front underside passes through the front ground bearer's top-front corner (`Y = 3.64`, `Z = 8.52`) and its upper edge butts the first-floor spandrel face (`Z = 8.485`); the side underside passes through the side ground bearer's top-outer corner (`|X| = 17.0`) and its upper edge butts the side spandrel face (`|X| = 16.965`). Clay flashing rolls cover both upper joints and meet in a corner ball; a clay roll covers the hip. Tile edges sit behind a 120 mm fascia that wraps the corner as a butt joint; 80 mm barge boards close the arch end and the rear end. Rafters (about 0.78 m centres) are seated in their bearers and flush with the tile underside, with one hip rafter carrying the corner. The side structure (bearer, spandrel, tie beam, floor edge) mirrors the front on the end plane and butts the corner pier.
- Stair core (Agent 06, `interior.ts` exports `STAIR`, `stepTop1/2`): a two-flight switchback centred on the entrance axis with a 0.4 m open well. 24 risers of 168.75 mm on 300 mm treads (11 treads per flight), 1.5 m wide. Flight 1 climbs toward -Z on the west (`X = -1.7..-0.2`, first riser `Z = -0.45`); the landing is 3.4 x 1.55 m at `Y = 2.775`, `Z = -5.3..-3.75`; flight 2 returns toward +Z on the east and arrives at the first floor at `Z = -0.45`. Both flights meet their landings by exactly one riser. Construction: stone treads with nosing lips on 184 mm plaster soffits whose top follows the riser feet; the landing is a stone deck on a plaster soffit, carried by a timber bearer on two round posts at `(+/-1.45, -5.05)`; no solid masses, 1.84 m clear under the landing.
- Stair balustrade: one slim timber system (80 x 65 mm handrail, 34 mm turned balusters at 150 mm centres standing on the tread or floor, 110 mm round newels). Raked rails sit 0.97 m above the nosing line and level rails 0.998 m above the floor; because every newel is set 50 mm behind a step edge, both rails arrive at identical heights (`0.998 = 0.97 + 0.05/0.3 x 0.16875`). Runs: flight 1 outer/inner (`|X| = 1.65 / 0.25`) from floor newels at `Z = -0.35` to landing newels at `Z = -3.80`; level landing rails on the west, rear (`Z = -5.25`) and east edges plus a short rail across the well; flight 2 outer/inner up to arrival newels at `Z = -0.40` on the first floor. No posts stand on treads or in the walking line.
- First-floor void: clean rectangle `X = -2.1..2.1`, `Z = -5.3..-0.45`, 0.4 m wider than the stair each side so the floor guard and the raked rail read as two lines. Guards at 0.998 m on the west, east and rear edges and on the two front segments either side of the arrival bay (`X = 0.2..1.7`), ending on the arrival newels; 40 mm teak edge band on the slab edges, stone nosing at the arrival. Headroom over the stair is the full upper storey; 3.79 m at the slab edge in front of flight 1.

## Isolated specialist handoffs

| Agent | Owned module | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Reference / blueprint | `blueprint.ts`, this document | Coordinates and opening schedule only |
| 2 Foundation / structure | `structure.ts` | Plinth, slabs, load-bearing wall segments with real voids, beams |
| 3 Entrance / main stairs | `entrance.ts` | Central arch, door, portico and exterior steps |
| 4 Windows / doors | `openings.ts` | Repeated timber opening inserts; never fills masonry voids |
| 5 Verandas / balconies | `verandas.ts` | Porch decks, posts, connected rails, supporting members |
| 6 Interior | `interior.ts` | U-stair and its guards only; both floors are open halls with no partitions |
| 7 Roof | `roof.ts` | Derived-datum hip/valley/ridge planes, gablet, eaves, fascia; exports `roofSurfaceHeight()` so attic closures and QA follow the roof |
| 8 Materials / aging | `materials.ts` | Shared PBR palette and restrained procedural texture atlases |
| 9 Gameplay / collision | `collision.ts` | Simplified walls and continuous walkable height fields |
| 10 Optimization | `optimization.ts` and cached primitives | Shared geometry, instanced balusters/shutters, textured (not modeled) roof tiles; silhouette protected |
| 11 Structural QA | `qa.ts` | Checks names, extents, symmetry, stair/route continuity |
| 12 Walkthrough QA | `collision.ts` route + viewer walkthrough | Outside to balcony and back |

The master integration is `heritageBuilding.ts`. Each module returns its own named group and never reparents or edits another module's mesh. The render application never exports scenery, cameras, or lights with the asset.

The downloadable package contains full-detail and mobile-detail GLBs, a separate collision GLB with `UCX_` blockers and `NAV_` floor/stair meshes, a collision JSON with the walkable-height rules, the shared blueprint JSON, optimization policy, and QA report. The visible GLBs intentionally omit the collision display material.

## Traversal contract

Exterior grade -> five real front steps -> central portico -> open double door -> central hall -> left/right room via openings in bearing walls -> hall -> first U-stair flight -> rear landing -> return flight -> first floor -> wing veranda through the middle wing doors -> central balcony through the central upper doorway -> stair and entrance back to grade. The upper slab has an actual stairwell void. Door leaves are held open for gameplay. Collision is a separate, lower-detail named hierarchy.
